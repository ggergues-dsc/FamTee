Connection points · top-down and left to right

renderer.js drawEdges · TB / LR

Every line starts and ends on a fixed point on the card, marked with a dot. Top-down: children enter at the top centre and partners join at the left or right middle. Left to right: children enter at the left middle and partners join at the top or bottom centre. Lines bend at right angles into these points, so a card that has been dragged a little off its row still connects cleanly instead of being joined at an angle. Pets (Biscuit and Miso) are their own kind of node: a rounder green card with an animal icon, hung from their owners’ partnership with a dotted line.

Connect people · try it

new · interaction.js + panel.js

Add someone before you know where they fit, then connect them later. + Person places a new person in the “Not connected yet” area. Hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and drag from one person to another, or drag the round handle on a card’s right edge. When you let go, choose how they are related. With the keyboard, focus a person, press C, then pick the other person. Esc cancels. The demo opens part-way through: Rosa has just been dragged onto David.
