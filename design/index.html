Connection points · top-down and left to right

renderer.js drawEdges · TB / LR

Every line starts and ends on a fixed point on the card, marked with a dot. Top-down: children enter at the top centre and partners join at the left or right middle. Left to right: children enter at the left middle and partners join at the top or bottom centre. Lines bend at right angles into these points, so a card that has been dragged a little off its row still connects cleanly instead of being joined at an angle. Pets (Biscuit and Miso) are their own kind of node: a rounder green card with an animal icon, hung from their owners’ partnership with a dotted line.

Tree view · View mode concept

proposal · read-only rendering of the same layout

In View mode the cards can turn into an actual tree, drawn from the same generations the layout already computes. Each founding couple is the trunk. Branches grow up toward descendants and get thinner as fewer people hang from them. People with children sit where a branch forks; people with no children yet are the leaves. Two families joined by a marriage are two trees whose branches meet and fuse, a graft, and that couple’s children grow from the shared branch. Three or more families sit side by side with a graft between each pair of trees that intermarry. They’re ordered the way the layout already orders blocks, so grafts stay short and branches don’t cross. The family switches at the bottom, next to the zoom buttons, show or hide each tree. The couple’s children stay while either of their families is shown, and the last family shown can’t be switched off.

Twins · proposal

cards, tree view and details

Twins usually share a surname and birth year, so on the cards they look like any two siblings. The proposal shows the bond in the shape of the tree, using the convention genealogists already know. Twins’ lines leave the family line from one point and split in a V. Identical twins get a short bar across the V. In the tree, twins grow from one shared twig. Identical twins fork from a single point into two mirror-image twigs, sitting level and linked. Fraternal twins fork unevenly, one branching off lower than the other. The data marks each twin’s link to the parents with the same birth group, plus an optional “identical” flag, so twins are never guessed from birth dates.

Person panel (Edit)

Connect people · try it

new · interaction.js + panel.js

Add someone before you know where they fit, then connect them later. + Person places a new person in the “Not connected yet” area. Hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and drag from one person to another, or drag the round handle on a card’s right edge. When you let go, choose how they are related. With the keyboard, focus a person, press C, then pick the other person. Esc cancels. The demo opens part-way through: Rosa has just been dragged onto David.
