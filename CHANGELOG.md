# Changelog

FamTee's versions (see [ADR 0020](docs/adr/0020-versions-and-release-labels.md)): a fix raises
the patch number, a feature the minor number, a change older apps can't follow the major number.

## 1.1.17 — 2026-10-16

- In the tree, founding couples now hang from their trunk on short twigs (one either side,
  growing up and out) instead of sitting on the trunk itself, with their names below them.

## 1.1.16 — 2026-10-16

- Fixed: in the tree, founding couples hung below the fork at the top of their trunk, so
  their limbs looked like branches growing downward. They now sit on the trunk, side by side
  with their names to either side, and every branch grows upward from the fork above them.

## 1.1.15 — 2026-10-16

The first numbered version. FamTee can now:

- Keep a family tree in one portable HTML file that works offline in any browser, with
  unsaved work kept in the browser and **Download** as the only way to write to disk
- Show the family as cards (Edit) or as an actual tree (View): trunks for founding couples,
  forks and leaves, grafts where families marry, family switches, groups, other relations
  and links to other graphs
- Record people with photos (two sizes), jobs and employers (with trade icons) and
  birthplaces (with country flags); pets of eight kinds; twins (fraternal and identical)
- Connect people later (Ctrl/⌘+drag, the + handle or C), with a relation chosen in a popover
- Export and import GEDCOM, and share a copy with living people's details removed
- Show this version in the corner of the canvas, with an About box; downloads record which
  version saved them
